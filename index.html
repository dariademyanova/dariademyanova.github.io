I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Management at the University of Bologna.

My research focuses on how complementors – third-party actors that co-create value in platform ecosystems – choose, time, and scale their platform commitments under various institutional and technological conditions. Across three papers of my dissertation, I study the institutional logics behind platform selection, the timing of complementors’ expansion during technological transitions, and their cross-border scaling after technological shocks, reframing complementors as strategic agents who shape ecosystem evolution.
